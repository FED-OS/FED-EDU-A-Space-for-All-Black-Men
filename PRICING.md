# 💰 FED-EDU PRICING — Rate Standards & Monetization Playbook

> The community's agreed standards for charging work, so no brother underprices himself out of desperation and no client lowballs the block. These are floors and guides — your skills set your ceiling.

## The Mindset

A brother with face tats and a felony record walks into a retail job interview — we all know how that goes. But a local business doesn't run a background check on a website that works. **You don't apply for permission in this economy; you ship value and charge for it.** The hustle-index exists so a client sees your badges, your live portfolio, and your rate — not your paperwork.

## Recurring Revenue (The Real Wealth)

One-off gigs pay bills; recurring pays futures. Every quote should include the maintenance retainer option. Ten clients at $50/month is $500/month arriving while you sleep — the quiet money that changes a brother's options. SaaS thinking at street scale: one useful tool, fifty small businesses paying $15/month for it, and you've built $750/month of digital rent. The blueprint library exists to make exactly that path repeatable.

## Charging Methods

**Ko-fi** (the community default — button snippet ready in `support-the-block/kofi-button.html`): lowest fees, no application gate, works for donations AND shop items. **Cash App / PayPal:** fine for local clients; keep records for yourself. **Crypto:** for international brothers where rails are hostile — your call, your risk tolerance. **Whatever the rail: get paid before final handoff.** 50% deposit on scoped work is standard and professional — say it like it's normal, because it is.

## Pricing Situations

**"But a kid on Fiverr will do it for $20."** Let him. Brothers who charge $20 for $500 work burn out and quit in a month; the client gets what $20 buys. Floors protect the ecosystem — a race to the bottom leaves the whole block poor.

**First client ever?** Charge the floor, deliver like it's $5,000, ask for a testimonial and permission to show the work. That testimonial is worth more than the check.

**Scope creep (client keeps adding "small favors")?** "That's outside what we scoped — happy to quote it." Say it early, say it friendly, say it every time. Scope in writing: what's included, what's not, how many revision rounds.

**A brother can't pay but the cause is real (church, youth program, a brother in crisis)?** Community-service work is honored here — log it in the Ship Yard as `#communitybuild` and it counts toward badges and tickets like paid work. Just budget your charity — free work still costs your hours.

**Clients outside the US?** Quote in their currency, get paid through Ko-fi/PayPal; remember brothers in Kingston and Lagos getting paid in USD or GBP is the entire point of the borderless hustle.

## Getting To Your First $1,000

2. Complete your `hustle-index/members/` file — flag, skills, floor-respecting rates, live links
3. List in the marketplace; get dapped by brothers who've seen your work
4. First client: local. Barbers, cleaners, cooks, churches, coaches, artists — the block's economy needs the block's tech
5. Deliver, collect, testimonial, referral ("know anybody else whose site is 2015?")
6. Stack one retainer at a time until the quiet money covers your bills — then you have options no gatekeeper can revoke
