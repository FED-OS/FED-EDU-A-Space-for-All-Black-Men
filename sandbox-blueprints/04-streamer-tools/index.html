    Chat-Safe Styling Rules

    Alerts are read aloud by screen readers and copy-pasted into clips. Three rules keep yours clean:

    1. Plain words over emoji soup. "SUB — TestBrother" beats an emoji wall. Emoji don't read the same on every phone, and clip re-uploads strip them.
    2. Name the human, not the handle, when you know it. Brothers follow the voice, not the tag. But never put a real name on the wall if the handle is anonymous — respect the mask.
    3. Cap the message length. Long alerts eat your dwell time and bury the next one in queue. The bot caps at 120 characters — same cap here.

    The Rungs

    Rung 1 — This page. Fire all four event types, tune dwell to your pace, screenshot the wall mid-pop for receipts.
    Rung 2 — Wire the bot. Open stream-alert-bot.js, follow the comments, connect it to your channel's event source (Twitch EventSub or Streamlabs socket). PR your working connection — 15 tickets.
