# ☕ Set Up Ko-fi — Take Payments Without a Bank Gatekeeping You

**Plain terms:** Ko-fi is the simplest way to take money online: a free page with a link, people tip or pay you, money lands in your PayPal/Stripe. No monthly fee, no application, no "we reviewed your business." For brothers without traditional banking, this is often the FIRST door — and it opens today.

## 🧠 Why Ko-fi and Not the Others

| Option | The gate |
|---|---|
| Stripe directly | Needs a bank account in good standing, business verification |
| PayPal Goods & Services for freelancing | Fees + dispute risk + account freezes on new sellers |
| CashApp/Venmo | Great for the block, but no storefront, no receipts, no tips page |
| **Ko-fi** | **An email address. That's basically it.** Connects to PayPal/Stripe later, but you start with the page.** |

Ko-fi = the storefront, CashApp/Venmo = the pipes. Start with Ko-fi's page, connect whichever pipe you actually have.

## 🪜 The Steps (15 minutes)

### Step 1 — Create the page
Go to `ko-fi.com` → **Sign up** (email + password). Choose your handle — this becomes your link: `ko-fi.com/yourhandle`. Same naming care as your GitHub username (see [Make a GitHub Account](../First-Steps/Make-a-GitHub-Account.md)): clean, professional, yours.

### Step 2 — Set the essentials
On your page setup: a short blurb ("Atlanta builder. Websites for local hustles, fixed fast and clean."), a photo or your logo, and your goal (optional). Keep it real — brothers can smell a fake story and they respect a true one.

### Step 3 — Turn on Shop (if you're selling)
Free Ko-fi includes basic Shop items. Add your first offering using the block's rate floors: "One-page site — $150" or "Website fix-up — $60/hr, $45 min." Every price you post publicly holds the floor for every brother behind you (see [Price Your Work](Price-Your-Work.md)).

### Step 4 — Connect the money pipe
Ko-fi → Settings → Payouts → connect PayPal (easiest start; prepaid PayPal accounts work for brothers without banks) or Stripe (needs a bank — add it when you have one; CashApp direct deposit counts as a bank for Stripe purposes for many users — verify for your situation).

### Step 5 — Put the link EVERYWHERE
- Your GitHub profile bio (first line)
- Your Hustle Index listing (`contact` field)
- Your site's footer
- Link-in-bio pages

The link only works where people see it.

## ⚠️ Real Talk

- **Ko-fi tips are "support," not "wages."** Money through Ko-fi can land as gifts/donations rather than formal invoices — great for tips and donations, less ideal for big client contracts. For $500+ client work, also learn a simple invoice habit (a Google Doc with line items, dates, and both names signed counts; a real invoice tool like Wave is free and does it cleaner).
- **Taxes are real.** Money in is income. Track every payment (the Ko-fi dashboard exports history — do it monthly). "I didn't know" doesn't work with the IRS, and building wealth means building clean books (see [PRICING.md](../../PRICING.md) — the recurring revenue section covers bookkeeping habits).
- **Never put your Ko-fi link in the main FED-EDU repo's donation button.** That button belongs to the block's own support fund (support-the-block/). Yours goes on YOUR pages and YOUR listings.
- **Watch out for fake "buyers."** Scammers DM new sellers with fake payment screenshots. Rule: money visible in YOUR account before work starts. No exceptions, no matter the story.
