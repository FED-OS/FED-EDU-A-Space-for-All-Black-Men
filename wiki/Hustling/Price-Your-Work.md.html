💰 Price Your Work — The Rate Floors and the "Never $75 for a Website" Talk

Plain terms: This page is the block's price protection. There's a floor under every service — the minimum you can charge without hurting yourself and every brother after you. Below the floor you're not "competing," you're training customers to undervalue ALL of us. Full table: PRICING.md. This page is WHY, and HOW to hold the line in a negotiation.

🧠 The Core Idea: Rate Floors, Not Rate Ceilings

A ceiling says "don't charge more than X" — nobody's capping your top end. Charge $5,000 for the same site if the client will pay it.

A floor says "never charge LESS than X" — because your floor is the next brother's ceiling. The client who paid you $75 for a website will use that number against every builder he meets for the next five years. One undercutter sets the block's market rate for a decade.

🗣️ How to Hold the Line (The Negotiation Scripts)

"That's too expensive — my nephew does websites for $50."

"Then you've got a great deal lined up. My work comes with a live portfolio, a maintenance option, and a real timeline. If the $50 site works out, take it — and when it doesn't, my price is the same and I'll still be here."

Calm. No shade on the nephew. The price doesn't move because somebody else undervalues their work.

"Can you do it for exposure?"

"Exposure doesn't cover my tools, my internet, or my time. The floor is $150 and it's firm. What I CAN do is a payment plan: half now, half on delivery."

"What if I give you repeat business?"

"Repeat business is exactly why the retainer exists — $50/month keeps me on call. The first job still starts at the floor."

The silence move: Quote the price, then STOP TALKING. Brothers negotiate against themselves by filling silence with discounts. The first one to blink pays for it. State the number. Wait.

🧮 The Math That Makes the Floor Obvious (Show a Client This)

When a client says "$150 is a lot for one page," walk them through what they're actually buying:

  • A professional online presence that works on every phone their customers own
  • Hosting included in the price (GitHub Pages, $0/mo — you pass nothing through)
  • A page that loads in under 3 seconds on bad connections (faster than most $2,000 sites)
  • 4-6 hours of skilled labor from a builder with a public portfolio

$150 ÷ 6 hours = $25/hr — below what a mechanic, plumber, or electrician charges. The floor is already the discount. The client isn't doing YOU a favor; you're doing them one.

🪜 The Ladder: Floors Go UP as You Level

Floors are for the block's baseline — your personal floor rises with your level (leaderboard levels in community-boards/):

  • Fresh Spawn: charge the floor exactly — build the portfolio, collect the vouches
  • Builder (1+ merged PR): floor + 20%
  • Certified Builder (5+ merged PRs): floor + 50%
  • OG (10+ merged PRs): you set your own rates — your GitHub does the talking

⚠️ Real Talk

  • Never race to the bottom with another brother. Two builders quoting the same job? Both quote the floor. The client picks on fit, not on who blinked. If a brother undercuts you, pull up on HIM (respectfully, in DMs): "Floor's $150, king — we eat together or we starve together."
  • The first client who pays full price changes your life more than ten discount clients. Full-price clients respect the work, refer equals, and come back. Discount clients demand, delay, and vanish.
  • The floor is a community rule, not just advice (see GOVERNANCE.md). Hustle Index listings below floor get a maintainer note first, removal if it stands. Not punishment — protection of the whole block's market.
  • Pro bono is ALLOWED and honored — but call it what it is. Building your mama's church site free? Beautiful. But in the invoice write "$150 — 100% community discount." The value is real even when the price is $0. That habit keeps your own head right about what your work is worth.
