## 🧭 Wiki House Rules

- **Plain talk only.** Jargon appears only WITH its translation (see urban-dictionary/).
- **Every page ends with "Your Move"** — one action under 10 minutes. Reading without doing is watching from the sidelines.
- **Phone-first.** Every page must read fine on a cracked 5-inch screen over 3G.
- **Brothers improve it.** See a page that's unclear? That's not your confusion — that's the page's bug. Fix it or flag it. Fixing wiki pages earns raffle tickets like any other contribution.

## ✏️ Editing This Wiki

The wiki lives in the repo (`wiki/` folder). To improve a page: fork → edit → PR. Typos count. Clarifying one confusing sentence counts DOUBLE — you're removing a wall that stopped every brother who read that page after you.

*Every master was once a disaster. The wiki is how we skip the years of getting lost. 🏁*
