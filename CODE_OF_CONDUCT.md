# 🛡️ FED-EDU CODE OF CONDUCT — The Ground Rules

## Our Standard

This is a safe house, a school, and a block all at once. Everyone who enters agrees to keep it that way. These rules exist so that a 12-year-old on his first bike and a 50-year-old brother on his second chance can build side by side without either one feeling out of place.

## The Six Rules

### 1. No question is stupid
If a brother doesn't know how to create a folder, save a file, or turn on the computer — we walk him through it. Zero judgment. Everyone here was once at square one. The brother who asks is the brother who levels up.

### 2. No jargon without translation
If you use a technical term, break it down. If you can't explain it in plain terms, you don't understand it well enough to teach it here. Point people to the urban-dictionary/ decoder when it helps.

### 3. Celebrate the attempts
The brother who pushed his first line of code gets the same dap as the brother who shipped an app. The first step is the hardest part of the tunnel. Post your firsts. Dap other people's firsts.

### 4. Zero tolerance for gatekeeping
Elitism, mocking, "RTFM" energy, downvote culture, "why are you even here" attitudes — banned. This is the anti-Stack Overflow. If you make beginners feel small, you're the problem, not them.

### 5. Ownership stays with the builders
Brothers who contribute to projects own their piece. Credit where credit is due — always. Nobody's work gets taken, renamed, and claimed by somebody else. Git history is our receipt.

### 6. Protect the younger brothers
Kids are in this space learning. Keep shared content appropriate for minors. No explicit content, no grooming, no directing young brothers toward anything unsafe. This rule has no appeal process — break it and you're gone.

## Who This Space Serves

All Black men. The Jamaicans, the Haitians, the Africans, the Americans, the British. The gay and trans brothers. The young and the elders. The incarcerated and returning citizens. The kid with his first bike and the man on his second chance. The homeless brother on library Wi-Fi. Whoever you are, if you're a Black man trying to learn, build, and grow — this block is yours.

And to be clear about who else is welcome: allies who genuinely grew up in and respect the culture, who follow these rules, and who understand this space exists because the mainstream ones left us out. We don't check melanin at the door; we check the vibe and the respect.

## Harassment & Discrimination

None. Not based on nationality, sexuality, gender identity, age, record, housing status, religion, disability, or skill level. A trans brother in London has the same seat at the table as a brother in Kingston. A returning citizen has the same seat as a college kid. If you can't respect that, the exit is the same door you came in.

## Violence & Illegal Activity

We don't glorify the streets — we understand them. Many of us come from that place. But this space is an exit, not a monument. No coordinating illegal activity, no weapons talk, no threatening anyone, inside or outside the community. The whole point is channeling that energy into the keyboard.

## Moderation

Maintainers moderate discussions, issues, and the hustle-index. Violations of rules 1–5 typically follow GOVERNANCE.md discipline (talk → mediation → sanctions). Rule 6 violations and anything endangering minors or members — instant removal and, where required, reporting to authorities.

## Enforcement Scope

These rules apply everywhere the FED-EDU flag flies: issues, discussions, DMs through the fed-comm system, wiki edits, project repos, chapter events, and anywhere a brother represents this community.

## The Bottom Line

We are taking the brilliant, sharp, hustling minds we've always had and pointing them at building. Treat every brother like he might build the next thing that changes all our lives — because he might. Give dap. Pass knowledge. Keep the door open behind you.

*Each one, teach one.*
