## What A Maintainer Commits To

1. **48-hour eyes** on issues and PRs touching your wing (community standard — see SUPPORT.md)
2. **Keep the docs true** — when reality drifts from documentation, docs get fixed fast
3. **Enforce the conduct rules** in your room — gatekeeping dies on sight, kids stay protected
4. **Stay lightweight** — every merge in your wing honors the 3-second rule
5. **Pass it forward** — spot and mentor your eventual replacement; rooms shouldn't die when life happens

## How Rooms Change Hands

Life happens — court dates, double shifts, family seasons. A maintainer stepping back opens a `[PROPOSAL]` discussion naming a successor (usually the most consistent contributor in that wing). Handover = keys documented, duties confirmed, AUTHORS.md updated. No drama, no vacuum — the block keeps running.

## Contacting Maintainers

**Code/docs:** open the issue or PR — public by default, that's how everyone learns. **Conduct/security:** direct and private per SECURITY.md. **"Who do I even ask?"** — Help Desk with the `custom` template and a maintainer will route you.
