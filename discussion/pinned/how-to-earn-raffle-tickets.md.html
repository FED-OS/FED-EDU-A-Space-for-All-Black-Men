🎟️ How to Earn Raffle Tickets (The Full Ledger)

(Pin this in Announcements. Money questions are the most-asked on any block.)

Plain terms: The block gives away refurbished PCs — real machines, shipped to your door, free. Entry costs nothing. You earn tickets by doing what you came here to do anyway: building, learning, and helping brothers. The ledger is public (physical-raffles/earn-tickets.json) and drawings are livestreamed. Nobody can quietly move the numbers — the ledger IS the trust.

🖥️ The Machines

  • Tier 1 (most common): Refurb business laptops — i5, 8GB RAM, SSD, fresh FED-OS install. Runs everything on this block comfortably.
  • Tier 2 (raffle finale, once a year): A genuinely good rig — the "you can build a career on this" machine.
  • Every machine ships with: FED-OS on the drive, the EasyTether course pre-loaded, and this block's README taped inside the box. A brother who never heard of us meets the whole block at once.
  • Winners are chosen by weighted random draw (every ticket = one entry) and announced live; the ledger and winner list stay public forever (physical-raffles/).

⚖️ The Rules That Keep It Clean

  1. Tickets are non-transferable and non-purchasable. You can't buy tickets, trade them, or stack them for anybody else. Earned only.
  2. Agent accounts don't play. Bots and agents (AGENTS.md) are excluded from the raffle — humans only.
  3. Self-verified ≠ verified. "Completed a guide" needs a maintainer or elder sign-off; code PRs count when MERGED, not when opened. The build.yml and leaderboard_sync.yml workflows are the neutral scorekeepers.
  4. One account per brother. Multiple accounts stacking tickets = all accounts disqualified, permanently. The raffle committee's call is final.
  5. Past a certain point, OGs step back. Brothers holding 3x the median ticket count get bumped to "sponsor status" — they still build, but their tickets convert to giving a newcomer a better draw weight. OGs who do this get honored on the leaderboard, not penalized. (Elders Council wrote this rule; it's how the block stays a ladder, not a leaderboard of the same winners.)

🗓️ Drawing Cadence

  • Every quarter: Tier 1 machine drawing (first one: see ROADMAP.md v1.0 milestone — target Q2 2027)
  • Annually: The Tier 2 finale drawing at the block's anniversary
  • Notifications: Announcements category — winners get tagged, shipping is arranged by DM, addresses are handled privately and deleted after shipping (see SECURITY.md — we collect the minimum, we keep nothing)

❓ Common Questions

"I'm phone-only — can I still win?" YES — and the machine finds you at the address you give for shipping. Phone-only brothers often have PRIORITY in the draw weight because the machine changes their situation the most. The committee weights for impact, not just tickets.

"Do tickets expire?" No. They carry between drawings. Your work is never wasted.

"Can I donate a machine to the raffle?" That's the highest honor on the block. DM a maintainer or drop it in Ideas — donor brothers get permanent honor on the leaderboard and 25 bonus tickets for the NEXT drawing (sponsor-status brothers can still win — just not twice in a row).

"What if I win and don't need it?" Pass it forward. Winner-who-redonates gets 25 tickets + the block's respect. We keep a public "chain of giving" list — some of those machines will change hands three times before they die, and every hand is logged.

Every ticket in that ledger is a brother deciding his future is worth the work. Build. Earn. Eat. 🏁
