# 🏁 Welcome to the Block — Start Here

*(Pin this in **Announcements**. This is the front door.)*

You made it. Whether you got here from a phone in bed, a library computer, a cousin's link, or a raffle machine sitting on your desk right now — you're in. This post is the map.

## The Three Beliefs This Block Runs On

1. **You are not behind.** Starting at 30, 40, 50 with a 2011 laptop and a cracked phone? The internet doesn't check your hardware. It checks your commits.
2. **No stupid questions.** The question you're scared to ask is the one five brothers are DMing about privately. Ask it out loud — you're doing them a favor.
3. **Ownership stays with you.** Everything you build here is yours. Forks are yours. Projects are yours. The block doesn't tax your hustle — ever.

## ⚖️ The Six Rules (Short Version — Full Law in CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

1. No stupid questions.
2. No untranslated jargon — translate or link the Urban Dictionary.
3. Celebrate attempts, not just wins.
4. Zero gatekeeping — ahead ≠ better.
5. Ownership stays with builders.
6. Protect the younger brothers. No appeal on this one.

## 🎟️ The Money Part (Because Brothers Ask)

Every real contribution earns raffle tickets toward free refurb PCs: merged PR = 5, completed guide = 10, full blueprint = 15, closed bug with receipts = 3. Full ledger: [physical-raffles/](../physical-raffles/). Drawings are livestreamed on a public ledger. **Nobody on this block pays to learn — ever.**

## 🫂 One Last Thing (The Real Part)

A lot of brothers come here carrying heavy things — records, evictions, grief, hopelessness, the feeling of being late to a life everybody else started on time. That's not a footnote here; that's the BLOCK. Post in General when it's heavy. Somebody's been where you are. And if a night gets dark, the numbers are in SUPPORT.md — 988 in the US, 116 123 in the UK. Real numbers, real people, 24/7.
