# 🗺️ Introduce Yourself (The Official Thread)

*(Pin this in **Introduce Yourself**. Brothers post their intros as comments/replies on this thread so they're all findable in one place.)*

This is the block's roll call. Copy the short template below (or the full one in `discussion/templates/new_member_intro.md`), paste it as a comment, and make it yours. The welcome agent greets every intro within 24 hours, and elders vouch for active brothers when opportunities come through the block.

## The Short Template (Two Lines Is Enough)

```
City/Area: ______
Mission: ______
```

That's a valid intro. "Atlanta. Trying to build my first website so I can leave this warehouse job." — perfect. Done. Posted.

## The Full Template (If You Want to Go Deeper)

```
Name or handle: ______
City/Area + country: ______
Age range (optional): ______
Hardware: phone-only / scrap PC + tether / FED-OS machine / library / real rig
Where you're at in tech: never touched it / tinkerer / building / working in it
What you want from the block: ______
What you can give the block: ______
One thing about you that has nothing to do with tech: ______
```

## How the Block Responds to Intros

- **Welcome agent** (automated): posts a greeting + your matching setup path within 24 hours
- **Elders & maintainers**: pull up on new brothers — especially phone-only and returning citizens — because those are the brothers the world counts out and this block counts IN
- **Brothers**: dap each other's missions. If somebody's building what you're building, link up — study groups are how most of us survive the hard parts
- **Vouching**: complete work with or for another brother, and the vouch counts when hustle leads come through the block

## House Rules for This Thread

- **No credentials required.** "Never touched code" is a completely valid intro — arguably the most respected one here, because it takes the most courage to post.
- **No flexing on fresh spawns.** A brother posting 20 years of experience does NOT respond to a fresh spawn's intro with "you should really know X first." That's gatekeeping; see Rule 4 and the moderation ladder in CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md.
- **Privacy is yours.** Post as much or as little as you want. First name, handle only, city or just state — whatever keeps you comfortable. Nobody here verifies your identity; we verify your builds.
- **The line that always hits:** "I'm doing this for my son/nephew/little brother." If that's your mission, say it — the block shows up different for fathers and uncles.
