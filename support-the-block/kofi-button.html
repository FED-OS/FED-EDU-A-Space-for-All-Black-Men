How To Use This Block

1. Copy the CSS block between the two marker comments into your stylesheet (or keep the <style> tag and paste the whole thing).

2. Copy the HTML block between the two marker comments where you want the button.

3. Make a Ko-fi account at ko-fi.com and replace YOUR_USERNAME in the link — it appears in exactly ONE place in the HTML (the href).

4. Your Ko-fi goal should say what the money is for: raffle machines, shipping costs, data stipends for phone-only brothers. People give to a mission, not a tip jar.

NOTICE: This template page is on the build's whitelist. The page YOU paste the block into is not. If YOUR_USERNAME is still in the href when your page hits main, the build fails with your file named — swap it or it doesn't ship.

Why no JavaScript? A link is faster than a script on tethered 3G. The 3-second rule from the playground applies to money buttons too. Plain anchor, plain CSS, works on every phone on Earth including the ones on EasyTether.
