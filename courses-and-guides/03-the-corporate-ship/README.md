# THE CORPORATE SHIP — SAIL FROM THE BLOCK TO THE BUILDING

This course is the bridge: the skills you build in the sandbox are real, and this
ship shows you how to walk them into a room where they pay for them. Whether the
room is an interview, a client call, or your own LLC paperwork — same cargo,
same ship.

**The cargo you're carrying:** every blueprint you fork, every guide you finish,
every commission you ship is a receipt. The Corporate Ship teaches you to stack
receipts into a resume that speaks manager and a rate card that speaks money.

## THE TWO GUIDES

**markdown-resume-builder.md** — the resume builder. Your resume becomes a
markdown file in your own repo: version-controlled, always current, deployable
as a page any recruiter can click. The guide walks the whole build — structure,
wording, what to cut, what to keep — and shows you how the same file feeds the
hustle-index listing later.

**interview-cheat-sheet.md** — the cheat sheet. Not fake answers — real frames.
The questions interviewers actually ask, what they're really checking for, and
how a brother who learned on a tethered phone answers them with receipts
instead of credentials. Plus the questions YOU ask back, because an interview
runs both directions.

## WHO THIS SHIP IS FOR

The young brother with skills but no paper trail. The older brother coming home
who needs the second chance to land right. The brother already freelancing who
wants the corporate salary conversation to go his way. If you've shipped
anything in this repo — playground build, blueprint rung, paid commission —
this course turns that work into an offer letter.

## THE FLOW

Finish a build in the sandbox or a course guide → open the resume builder →
turn the build into a bullet point with numbers on it → drill the cheat sheet →
walk in the room. When the offer or the client lands, log it on the
hustle-index and the community boards. The receipts stack, the rate rises.

## THE HONEST PART

Nobody's promising a job in 30 days. We promise the opposite of a promise: a
worked system, in the open, with the receipts culture behind it. The brother
who does the reps ships. The brother who waits for a callback that never comes
doesn't. Same as the gym, same as the block: the work is the luck.
