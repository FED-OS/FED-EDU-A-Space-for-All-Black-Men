⚡ The 60-second pitch

FED-EDU = (Urban Dictionary + GitHub) × FED-OS × fedpromptly.

We get Black men into AI, coding, and tech — ALL of us: Jamaican, Haitian, African, American, British; gay and trans brothers; young and old; locked up and coming home; homeless and rebuilding; kids who need somewhere to be. Not by melanin alone — a white boy who grew up on this block is more "of it" than a stranger with the darkest skin. The block is culture and love, not color.

Hardware is never the disqualifier: phone-only brothers are full members. Scrap-PC brothers run EasyTether. The site obeys the 3-second rule — every page loads fast on tethered 3G, because that's who we serve first.

Kunta Kinte had his name beaten out of him. MLK left us an economic blueprint. Obama showed the system can be mastered. We're the chapter they never got to write.
