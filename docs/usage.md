# 📖 FED-EDU USAGE — How To Use The Platform Day-To-Day

> INSTALL.md gets you set up. This file is how you LIVE here — the daily flow, the rhythms, the plays.

## Your First Week

**Day 1 — Claim your name.** GitHub account (wiki/First-Steps/Make-a-GitHub-Account.md), join the org, follow the org and the brothers in the directory. Drop your intro in the Spawn Zone: your flag 🇯🇲🇭🇹🇳🇬🇬🇧🇺🇸, your hood or city, and your lane (music / sports / design / streaming / still figuring it out).

**Day 2 — Get online, whatever it takes.** Phone-only is a valid setup here. Run the EasyTether Mastery course if you've got a scrap PC needing a connection. Library Wi-Fi counts. Data budgets are respected everywhere — the whole platform stays light.

**Day 3 — First push.** Fork a blueprint, change one line, push it. That's the hardest step of the whole tunnel, and it earns your first badge. Post it. Take your dap.

**Day 4 — Pick your lane.** Browse the sandbox-blueprints. Music → Rap Lab. Ball → StatTracker. Design → Media FX. Streaming → Streamer Tools. Or just start a Flex Page website about whatever you love. Real-Life Minecraft rules: build what YOU want.

**Day 5 — Meet the block.** Answer somebody's `[NEED-PLUG]` if you can, even if you just learned the fix yesterday. Ask your own question without shame. Read one wiki page and one excellence-matrix entry.

**Day 6 — Learn the AI coach.** Set up the fedpromptly bridge and the prompts/ library. 3AM, script broken, no humans awake — the coach pulls up. (Double-check everything it hands you. AI makes mistakes.)

**Day 7 — Set your sights.** Read ROADMAP.md and todo.md. Claim a task or start your own project. You're in the flow now.

## The Daily Flow (After Week One)

**Log in → check three places → build:**

1. **The Help Desk** — issues tagged `[NEED-PLUG]` and `[STUCK-IN-THE-LAB]`. Answer one if you can. Being helped and helping are the same muscle.
2. **The Ship Yard** — brothers posting live builds. Test them, dap them, steal ideas shamelessly (that's what MIT license means).
3. **Your own project** — even 20 minutes a day compounds. One small brick daily beats one heroic weekend a month.

**Weekly:** ship something, even tiny — a guide edit, a term decoded, a blueprint tweak. Post it in the Ship Yard. Consistency is the whole secret — every "overnight success" in tech was somebody boring-consistent for two years first.

## Earning & Leveling

**Badges** land automatically when you push (auto_dap.yml workflow). They stack on your GitHub profile — your trophy room that recruiters can see.

**Raffle tickets** accrue from verified activity (push code = 5 tickets, complete a guide = 10, help a brother = 2 — full metrics in physical-raffles/earn-tickets.json). Redeem nothing — they just accrue until a raffle drops. A refurbished desktop might land at your door. Hope you can hold in your hands.

**The hustle-index** is for when you're ready to charge. Build a portfolio first (3+ shipped projects), then complete your member file and list. See PRICING.md for rate standards the community agreed on.

## Tools You'll Touch

- **The dashboard** (`core-system/`) — the front door; installable as an app on any phone or desktop via the PWA manifest
- **The DM system** (`fed-comm-dm/`) — brother-to-brother, encrypted client-side, never asks for your password
- **The decoder** (`urban-dictionary/`) — tech words → plain terms, searchable from a phone
- **The AI coach** (`fedpromptly-coach/` + `prompts/`) — instant plain-language help
- **The matrix** (`excellence-matrix/`) — when you need to remember whose shoulders you stand on

## Etiquette In The Wild

- **Dap generously.** Somebody's first push is their hardest one. Be the brother who shows up in the comments.
- **Share the receipts.** "I got my first paid commission" posts help the next brother believe it. Money talk is welcome here — that's the point.
- **Respect the data budget.** Compress images, keep files light, lazy-load heavy stuff. Half this community is on metered connections.
- **No shame in any lane.** The 11-year-old modding Minecraft and the 54-year-old building a barber-shop scheduler are both exactly where they're supposed to be.
- **Pass it on.** Somebody walked you through your first fork. When you're six months deep, walk the next brother through his.

## When Life Happens

Miss a week, a month, a season — the block doesn't expire. Brothers have court dates, shifts that run double, family crises, stretches where survival eats all the bandwidth. Come back when you can; the org will be here. Your badges stay, your history stays, your seat stays.

## The Exit Strategy (The Real One)

The whole point of this platform: a brother who masters it stops depending on anyone's permission — no hiring manager, no background check, no gatekeeper. The progression is real: learn → build → ship → charge → own. Plenty of brothers will use FED-EDU skills inside great jobs. Plenty will build their own tables. Both are wins. The mission is that every brother who walks in leaves with options he never had before.

That's usage. Now go build something.
