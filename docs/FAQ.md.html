❓ FED-EDU FAQ — Real Questions, Plain Answers

Q: Do I need to know how to code already? A: No. Zero. This space was built specifically for the true beginner — the brother who doesn't know what a file is yet gets walked through it. If you can explain how to run a play, you can write code. Start: wiki/First-Steps/.

Q: I only have a phone. Can I still participate? A: Fully. Phone-only is a valid, first-class setup here. GitHub's app and website let you edit code right from a phone screen, and the dashboard installs as an app. Plenty of brothers started exactly there.

Q: I'm not from the streets — is this space still for me? A: Yes. Street terms are our translation layer, not a membership card. Jamaican, Haitian, African, British, American; gay, trans, young, elder; student, worker, returning citizen, homeless brother — all Black men, all welcome. Cultural translation, not a box. See CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md.

Q: I have a criminal record. Does that matter here? A: Not to a compiler. The machine only asks if the code works. And the hustle-index route means clients see your portfolio and badges, not your paperwork. Plenty of brothers here have records — that's partly why this exists.

Q: Is this free? A: Everything costs $0: hosting, tools, courses, blueprints, community. Optional Ko-fi fuel funds desktops and data plans for brothers who need them. You will never be paywalled out of learning here.

Q: Is this only for beginners? What if I'm already skilled? A: Already-skilled brothers are the mentors and maintainers — the ones who answer Help Desk issues and keep wings running. There's a standing rule: the community needs your knowledge as much as you once needed somebody's. Plus the hustle-index pays at every level.

Q: Why GitHub? Why not a normal website with a forum? A: Because the platform is the curriculum — navigating FED-EDU teaches professional git/GitHub skills by immersion, and your public profile becomes your portfolio automatically. ADR-002 has the full reasoning.

Q: The street metaphors — isn't that stereotyping? A: The mission is the opposite: MTV airing rap videos never assumed every Black person lived that reality — but it refused to pretend the culture didn't exist. We meet brothers where they are with language they recognize, without assuming everyone's the same. If a metaphor doesn't fit you, skip it; the knowledge works in any dialect.

Q: Can I make money from this? How? A: That's the entire point. Build a portfolio → list on the hustle-index → get hired at community-floor rates (PRICING.md) → stack retainers. The path: learn → build → ship → charge → own. No gatekeeper holds a veto on it.

Q: Can I build a business on these blueprints? Own it completely? A: Yes — 100%. MIT License: fork a blueprint, build your empire, keep every right. Only rule: keep the license notice so the next brother can find this place too. COPYING.md in plain terms.

Q: What's FED-OS? What's fedpromptly? How do they relate to FED-EDU? A: FED-OS is the lightweight operating environment (runs on anything); fedpromptly is the AI engine. FED-EDU is the educational wing — the school and community that plugs into both. The equation: (Urban Dictionary + GitHub) × FED-OS × fedpromptly.

Q: Is the AI coach trustworthy? A: The coach is a 3AM first responder — instant, plain-language, patient. But AI makes mistakes, so the culture is verify-before-ship: test what it hands you. We teach that from day one. ADR-009.

Q: Are kids safe here? A: That's Rule 6 — protecting younger brothers — and it has zero appeal process. Moderation is active; violations get removed instantly and reported where the law requires. Parents: read CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md and SECURITY.md.

Q: How do the PC raffles work? A: Verified activity earns tickets (push code = 5, guide completed = 10 — full table in physical-raffles/). Raffles draw winners transparently from the public ledger. Winners get a refurbished desktop shipped, checklist-verified. Hope, made physical.

Q: Can women participate? A: This space was built to fill a specific vacuum: Black men missing from tech spaces. Our sister organizations (blackcomputeHER, Black Girls Code, others in CITATIONS.md) serve Black women with their own dedicated rooms — we link and collaborate rather than duplicate. Allies are welcome to follow the Code of Conduct and contribute.

Q: What if somebody disrespects me here? A: Report to any maintainer — privately, immediately (SUPPORT.md). Disputes follow GOVERNANCE.md: talk → mediation → sanctions. Harassment over nationality, sexuality, gender identity, age, record, or housing status doesn't get a second chance.

Q: I'm locked up. Can I really do this? A: Brothers inside have used prison education programs and reentry resources to start learning; on release, the block is waiting with $0 barriers. The wiki keeps everything self-paced for exactly this. A record can't stop software from running.

Q: How do I actually join? A: GitHub account → request into the org → drop your intro in the Spawn Zone (flag, city, lane) → push one change for your first badge. INSTALL.md walks all of it on any hardware you have.

Q: Who runs this? A: The community, under GOVERNANCE.md — founders protect the mission, maintainers keep the wings, members vote the direction, elders hold the culture. The block is designed to outlive any individual.

Q: Why 'FED-EDU'? A: FED — the family brand (FED-OS, fedpromptly). EDU — education. The educational wing of the ecosystem, built as the front door for brothers the rest of tech keeps outside.
