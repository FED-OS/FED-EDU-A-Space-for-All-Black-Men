# 🆘 FED-EDU SUPPORT — How To Get Help

> Stuck? That's what the block is for. No question is stupid here — we mean that literally. Here's exactly where to go depending on what you need.

## Quick Answers

| You need... | Go here | Speed |
|---|---|---|
| A tech word decoded | `urban-dictionary/` search | Instant |
| Help with code that won't work | Help Desk issue `[NEED-PLUG]` | Usually same day |
| Guidance on what to build | Discussion: Spawn Zone | Same day |

## The Help Desk (Primary Channel)

The fastest way to get unstuck. Open a GitHub issue and pick the template that fits:

- **`[NEED-PLUG]`** — your code is broken or won't run and you need a fix. Paste the code, describe what you expected vs. what happened, and say what you're running it on (phone / old desktop / FED-OS). Screenshots help if you can.
- **`[STUCK-IN-THE-LAB]`** — you understand the goal but can't figure out the next move. Bigger-picture help: which path to take, what to learn next, how to structure a project.
- **`bug_report.md`** — something in FED-EDU's own tools is broken (dashboard, DM system, blueprints).

A brother — maintainer or member — will pull up. Nobody gets mocked for the level of their question. That's Rule 1.

## The Spawn Zone (Community Wisdom)

For questions that don't have one right answer: "which language should I start with?", "is this project idea crazy?", "how do I stay motivated when everything's falling apart?" Post in the discussions under Spawn Zone. Brothers who've been where you are will talk it through with you.

## The Logic Wiki (Self-Serve, 24/7)

`wiki/` holds the accumulated knowledge of the block, written in plain terms. Getting-Online covers tethering and free access points. First-Steps covers accounts, forks, and first pushes. Building covers websites, apps, and publishing. Hustling covers listings, payments, and pricing. If the wiki's missing something you needed, that's a contribution opportunity — write the page you wish existed.

## The AI Coach (fedpromptly)

When no human's awake at 3AM and your script won't run, the AI coach bridge in `fedpromptly-coach/` (plus the `prompts/` library) is there. Feed it your broken code with the debug_helper prompt. It explains the fix in plain terms and tells you exactly where to paste it. Remember: AI can make mistakes — always double-check what it hands you before shipping.

## Response Times

The community operates on mutual aid, not SLAs. That said, the culture here is fast: most Help Desk issues get eyes within hours, and maintainers are expected to keep their wings answered within 48. If something sits longer, tag a maintainer from `MAINTAINERS.md`.

## Private Matters

Code problems are public (that's how everyone learns). Personal matters aren't:

- **Harassment or conduct violations:** message any maintainer directly or use the contact in `SECURITY.md`. Handled immediately and discreetly.
- **Mental health & life crises:** this community is a brotherhood, not a clinic. Brothers will talk with you, but please also reach out to real help — in the US, call or text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). In the UK, Samaritans at **116 123**. In Jamaica, **888 NEW LIFE**. In Nigeria, **0806 000 0000** (SURPIN). Keep fighting. The tunnel has an end.

## Before You Ask — The 60-Second Checklist

1. Did you search the wiki and urban-dictionary first?
2. Did you search closed issues? Somebody might've hit the same wall.
3. Can you paste the exact code and the exact error? (The words on the screen, copy-pasted, not summarized from memory)
4. Can you say what you expected to happen vs. what happened?

Bring those four things and you'll get a real answer fast. Even if you can't — ask anyway. That's the rule.

## Giving Support

The strongest contribution a brother can make: answer one Help Desk issue a week. Somebody once walked you through something. Pass it forward. Each one, teach one.
